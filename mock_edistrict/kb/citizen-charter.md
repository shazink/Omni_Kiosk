# Citizen Charter: Kerala e-District Civic Services

## 1. Income Certificate
- **Purpose**: Required for availing educational scholarships, fee concessions in schools/colleges, housing schemes, and medical welfare benefits.
- **Definition of Annual Income**: Annual family income means the total aggregate income earned by the applicant and all living family members residing under the same household during the preceding 12 months. It includes salary, daily wages, agricultural earnings, business profits, pensions, and rental income.
- **Income Limits**:
  - Educational Scholarships: Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh) per year.
  - Higher Education Fee Concessions: Limit is Rs. 2,50,000 per year.
  - Housing Schemes: Limit varies by scheme (typically Rs. 1,50,000 per year).
- **Processing Time & SLA**: 7 working days from date of online submission and document verification by the Village Officer.
- **Validity Period**: An Income Certificate is valid for exactly 1 (one) year from the date of issuance.
- **Competent Authority**: Tahsildar / Village Officer of the relevant Revenue Village.

## 2. Disability Pension
- **Eligibility**: Citizens residing in Kerala with a certified physical, sensory, or intellectual disability of at least 40%.
- **Required Documents**: Disability Assessment Certificate issued by an authorized District Medical Board, Aadhaar card, and Active Bank Passbook.
- **Monthly Pension Amount**: Rs. 1,600 per month deposited directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **Processing Window**: 14 working days following physical document scrutiny by the Social Welfare Department.

## 3. Community Certificate
- **Purpose**: Required for affirmative action, competitive examinations, admissions, and governmental employment reservations.
- **Validity**: 3 years for OBC/OEC non-creamy layer certificates; lifetime validity for SC/ST verification subject to statutory rules.
- **Processing Time & SLA**: 5 working days from submission.

## 4. General Kiosk Instructions
- **Self-Declaration**: If formal salary slips are unavailable, informal workers may submit a self-declaration affidavit countersigned by local ward member.
- **Tracking**: Every application receives a tracking number starting with `KL-EDIST-2026-` upon submission.
